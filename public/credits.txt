Parkline asset credits

The playable Thar-style 4x4 and GT Sports Coupé are original Parkline
models made from the supplied vehicle references. Roof, body paint,
interior and wheels are separate parts for customization and animation.

NVIDIA, SimReady Assets, CC BY 4.0
https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source packages: environment_floor_a01, barrier_wall_plastic_orange_a03,
structure_wall_straight_a01.
Changes: converted OpenUSD geometry to GLB, simplified geometry, cropped
the barrier to its base, reprojected UVs, optimized the source textures,
and rescaled the models for the original lot.

https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Interface cues were converted to MP3 for browser playback.

Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven, Tree Small 02, CC0 1.0
https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02
Changes: simplified trunk and branches, baked the leaf sprigs into a
cutout texture atlas, and fitted scale and rotation to the parking verge.
